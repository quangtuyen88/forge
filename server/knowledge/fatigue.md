# Fatigue

## Fatigue: score formula

Daily fatigue score (0–100) = 0.35 × acute:chronic volume ratio score + 0.25 × soreness score (1–5 check-in, scaled) + 0.20 × sleep deficit score (HealthKit vs 7-day baseline) + 0.20 × missed RPE penalty (sessions where RPE_actual > target + 1).

## Fatigue: ACVR bands

ACVR = (last 7 days volume) / (avg weekly volume, last 28 days). ACVR 0.8–1.3 → score 0 (green). ACVR 1.3–1.5 → score 50 (yellow). ACVR > 1.5 → score 100 (red, injury-risk zone).

## Fatigue: interventions

Fatigue 0–39: proceed as planned. Fatigue 40–59: proceed but reduce optional/back-off sets by 1. Fatigue 60–79: convert today to a light session, −30% volume, cap RPE 7. Fatigue 80–100: force a rest day or trigger an early deload; notify the user with an explanation.
