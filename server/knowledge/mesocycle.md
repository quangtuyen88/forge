# Mesocycle

## Mesocycle: accumulation weeks

Week 1 starts at MEV per muscle. Weeks 2–5 add 1–2 sets per muscle per week, approaching MAV. Week 6 is the deload, then the mesocycle repeats.

## Mesocycle: deload week

Week 6 is the deload week: 50% volume, 60–70% intensity, RPE ≤ 6. After the deload week, repeat the mesocycle from MEV.
