# Volume landmarks

Hard sets per week, per muscle group.

## Volume landmarks: how to read

MV = maintenance volume (minimum to keep muscle). MEV = minimum effective volume (least productive dose). MAV = max adaptive volume (top of the productive range). MRV = max recoverable volume (most you can recover from; above this, junk volume).

## Volume landmarks: chest

Chest needs 6 hard sets per week to maintain (MV), 8 as minimum effective volume (MEV), 12–16 for max adaptive volume (MAV), and 20 as max recoverable volume (MRV). Above MRV, chest work outpaces recovery.

## Volume landmarks: back

Back needs 8 hard sets per week to maintain (MV), 10 as minimum effective volume (MEV), 14–18 for max adaptive volume (MAV), and 22 as max recoverable volume (MRV).

## Volume landmarks: recovery reduction

Adjust MRV down ~15–20% for users flagging poor sleep (under 6h average) or high life stress in onboarding.
