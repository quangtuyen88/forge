# Substitutions

## Substitutions: shoulder flag

shoulder_flag swaps: barbell bench → dumbbell bench (neutral grip); overhead press → landmine press; dips → cable fly.

## Substitutions: knee flag

knee_flag swaps: back squat → leg press (high foot) or hack squat; lunges → leg extension (controlled).

## Substitutions: back flag

back_flag swaps: deadlift → hip thrust or cable pull-through; bent row → chest-supported row.

## Substitutions: volume credit

A substitution preserves the movement pattern's volume credit — the swap counts toward the same muscle's hard sets as the original exercise.
