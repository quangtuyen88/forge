# How program weeks work

Each Regulift training block has 6 program weeks, and week 6 is the deload week.

The program week counts completed workouts, not calendar weeks. It moves up one week each time you complete as many workouts as your plan has days per week, counted from the day the block started. For example, with 4 workouts a week, workouts 1 to 4 belong to week 1, workouts 5 to 8 to week 2, and workouts 9 to 12 to week 3. With 3 workouts a week, workouts 7 to 9 belong to week 3.

Days or weeks without training do not move the program week, so a missed week never skips you ahead. Changing how many days a week you train keeps you in the same program week. If you start an early deload, the program shows the deload week.

To check your own numbers, look at your current week and how many workouts you have completed since the block started.
