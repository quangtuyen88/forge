# Target RPE and reported RPE

RPE (rating of perceived exertion) rates how hard a set was on a 1 to 10 scale; 10 means you could not do another rep. Each planned exercise has a target RPE, the effort the plan aims for. Your reported RPE is the effort you enter after a set.

If you do not enter an RPE, Regulift keeps the effort unknown. It never fills in the target or a default for you, and the set shows its effort as Not entered.

What that changes: the set still counts as a recorded set in your training volume. Your average RPE uses only the sets you rated. If the last set of an exercise has no RPE, the next workout holds that exercise's load ("effort not recorded, load held") instead of raising or lowering it. The increase for reaching the top of the rep range needs a reported RPE on every set of that exercise.

To let the plan adjust your loads, enter an RPE for each set. You can add a missing one later by editing the workout in History.
