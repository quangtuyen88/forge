# When a plan change takes effect

You can change days per week, session length, split or goal. The coach prepares the change as a card, and nothing changes until you tap Apply plan changes on that card. You can also change these in Settings → Training.

An applied change starts with your next workout that has not been started. Workouts you already completed stay saved, and your program week does not restart.

A later start date, such as next Monday, is not supported yet. If you want the change to begin later, apply it when you are ready.

A prepared change can go out of date. If your plan changes in another way before you apply it, or 7 days pass, the card can no longer be applied; ask again and the coach prepares a fresh one. After you apply a change, you can undo it from the confirmation in the chat.
