Questions, answered.

Is Regulift medical advice?

No. Regulift is a training-programming tool, not a medical device. It never diagnoses or treats. If something hurts or you have a health condition, see a qualified professional.

Do I need an account?

No. Regulift works without one. You only sign in if you want sync across devices and crew features.

What does the coach see?

Your training log, your profile, and the notes you ask it to remember. It never sees your Health data, and on-device answers never leave your phone.

Which devices?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, plus the Apple Watch app. Apple Intelligence answers need iOS 26 and a supported iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Logging, your plan and your history work offline. The coach service needs a connection unless the on-device model is available.

Can I bring my history?

Yes. Import from Strong and Hevy via CSV, and export your data as CSV any time.
