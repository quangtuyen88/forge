Questions

Who is Forge for?

Intermediate lifters with 1–4 years of training who have outgrown static programs — 5/3/1, PPL spreadsheets — and want coaching logic without $200/month coach fees.

Is this medical advice?

No. Forge is a training tool. It refuses medical, injury-rehab and supplement-dosing questions and points you to a professional. Nothing here replaces a doctor or physiotherapist.

What happens to my data?

Your log lives on your device. Coach questions and your training context go to Forge's server (Cloudflare) to write the answer. Nothing is sold, there are no ads. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Training, logging and program math are fully on-device. Only the coach chat needs a connection.

Do I need a chest strap or wearable?

No. Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate from Apple Health sharpen the fatigue model, but check-ins alone work fine.
