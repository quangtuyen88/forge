# Exercise art

Each of the 314 exercises in `ForgeCore/Sources/ForgeCore/ExerciseDB.swift` has one illustration, `ex-<id>` in `App/Forge/Assets.xcassets/ExerciseArt`. The images are generated with the GPT image model through the Codex CLI and matched to one style anchor (`ex-back_squat`). The muscle colors come from the exercise's `primary` and `synergists`, so the art always agrees with the "Target muscles" map on the exercise sheet. `ExerciseArt` falls back to the data-driven `MuscleThumb` when an asset is missing.

## Style

- One adult figure, gender-neutral, bald, no clothes, no hair: an anatomy-chart écorché. Individual muscles are clearly separated with fine fiber lines, matte shading, thin dark outlines. Realistic human proportions.
- Colors, followed exactly:
  - PRIMARY muscle: solid royal blue #0062E6, flat and fully saturated, on both sides of the body.
  - SECONDARY muscles: pale sky blue #A8C8FF, clearly lighter than the royal blue.
  - Every other muscle, tendon and body part: neutral gray. No blue anywhere else. No other colors.
- Equipment: simple and correct for the exercise, in dark charcoal gray #3A3F4B, and only what the exercise needs.
- Background: flat pure white #FFFFFF. No floor line, shadow, gradient, vignette or border.
- Never any text, letters, numbers, labels, arrows, logos or watermarks.
- Composition: square 1:1. Figure plus equipment fill about 80 % of the canvas, centered, nothing cropped.
- Pose: the middle of one repetition, with anatomically correct joint angles, grip, stance and body position.
- Camera: a three-quarter front view, or a three-quarter rear view when the primary muscle is on the back of the body; a side three-quarter view from slightly above for lying exercises. The primary and every secondary muscle must be visible.

## Generate

`scripts/exercise-art-batches.py` writes one prompt per exercise. `--run N` generates every missing image with N parallel `codex exec` calls. `CODEX_BASE_URL` and `CODEX_MODEL` select the endpoint and model. A single id can be regenerated by deleting its PNG and passing the id.

```
python3 scripts/exercise-art-batches.py --run 10
```

## Verify

Every image is graded before import. It FAILs on any of these:
- PRIMARY: the primary muscle is not solid royal blue, or it is not visible.
- EXTRA: blue on a muscle that is not listed.
- SWAP: a secondary is solid, or the primary is pale.
- EQUIP: wrong or missing equipment.
- POSE: not this exercise, or a joint bends the wrong way.
- CROP: the figure or its equipment is cut by the frame.
- BG: the background is not flat white.
- ART: text, a watermark, extra or missing limbs, or deformed hands or feet.

A secondary muscle hidden by the view is a note, not a failure. Failed images are regenerated with a pose note until they pass.

## Import

Convert the verified PNGs to JPEG in one folder, then run `python3.13 scripts/import-exercise-art.py <folder>`. It whitens the background and writes 600 px imagesets.
